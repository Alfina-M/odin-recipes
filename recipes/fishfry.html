Fish Fry

Description

Fish Fry is a crispy and flavorful South Indian dish made by marinating fish with spices and shallow frying it until golden and delicious.

Ingredients

  • 500 g Fish (any firm fish)
  • 1 tbsp Ginger Garlic Paste
  • 1 tsp Turmeric Powder
  • 1 tsp Chili Powder
  • 1 tsp Pepper Powder
  • 1 tsp Garam Masala
  • 1 tbsp Lemon Juice
  • Salt to taste
  • 2 tbsp Rice Flour
  • 2 tbsp Oil (for frying)
  • Curry Leaves (optional)

Steps To Make Fish Fry

  1. Clean the fish. Wash the fish pieces thoroughly and pat them dry.
  2. Prepare the marinade. In a bowl mix ginger garlic paste, turmeric powder, chili powder, pepper powder, garam masala, lemon juice, and salt.
  3. Marinate the fish. Apply the spice mixture evenly on the fish pieces. Let it rest for 30 minutes for better flavor.
  4. Add coating. Sprinkle rice flour over the marinated fish and coat lightly to get a crispy texture.
  5. Heat oil. Heat oil in a pan on medium flame.
  6. Fry the fish. Place the fish pieces gently in the pan. Fry each side for 4–5 minutes until golden brown and crispy.
  7. Add curry leaves. Optionally add curry leaves while frying for extra aroma.
  8. Drain excess oil. Remove the fish and place on paper towel.
  9. Serve. Serve hot with lemon wedges, onion slices, and mint chutney.
